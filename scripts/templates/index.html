Results

Everything below is computed from the scores recorded for each trial. A model whose run failed is not shown, and a trial that filed no report is left out; each model's info tooltip gives its date, scored trials, and any trials without a report. Each bar carries a 95% Wilson score interval, drawn as the line across it and given as the grey range under its value.
